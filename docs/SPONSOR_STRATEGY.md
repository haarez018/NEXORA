## Sequencing and boundaries
Keep sponsor SDKs out of Phase 1. PayPal is the intended Phase 7 integration; Render is a deployment candidate, not a selected host. Channel3 and Bryntum are optional extensions requiring a clear user benefit and a separate implementation decision. Do not replace Prisma/PostgreSQL, deterministic policy, or existing module boundaries solely to add a sponsor.

No sponsor secrets belong in client code, screenshots, commits, logs or demo videos. Bryntum licensing requirements depend on the chosen product/edition; establish permitted demo/submission redistribution before adding packages. Do not claim installed dependencies, successful calls, active deployment or prize eligibility in this document.
